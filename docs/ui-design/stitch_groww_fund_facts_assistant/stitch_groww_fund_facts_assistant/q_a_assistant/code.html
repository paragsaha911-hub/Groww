Is there any lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax saver?

10:44 AM
HDFC ELSS Tax Saver
Statutory Clause

Yes. HDFC ELSS Tax saver has a statutory lock-in period of 3 years (36 months) from the date of allotment under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961. Redemption or switching is strictly prohibited prior to completion of 3 years.

lock_clock

In SIP purchases, each individual installment carries its own independent 36-month lock-in window from its specific allotment date.
